01:56By design

Signals, not verdicts.

Baseline
Every voice is compared with itself first. Age, accent, language and health all change how people sound, so one universal threshold would be unfair.
Human review
A signal moves a call up the queue. It never accuses or decides. An investigator listens and makes the call.
False alarms
Some signals will be wrong. Scores should always travel with the evidence behind them, never as certainties.
Consent and law
Built for lawful investigation, with access controls, audit trails and data kept no longer than needed as the standard to hold it to.
