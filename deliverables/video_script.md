# Dhvani: Official Project Showcase Film Script
**Target Duration:** ~3 minutes 30 seconds  
**Narrative Tone:** Measured, authoritative, cinematic documentary.  
**Style Reference:** Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer* pacing meets Apple Keynote product reveals.

---

### ACT I: THE ANATOMY OF SILENCE (0:00 – 0:35)

**Visual:**  
Pitch black frame (`#050506`). Slow-motion pulsing crimson audio waveform expanding in 2.39:1 CinemaScope letterboxing.  
A real dispatch audio frequency scan flickers across the center.  
Text fades in with subtle tracking: *"IN AN EMERGENCY, SECONDS DECIDE SURVIVAL."*

**Sound Design:**  
45 Hz sub-bass rumble. Deep 60 BPM human heartbeat pulse. Faint radio dispatch static in the background.

**Narration (Authoritative, serious, deliberate):**  
*"Every day across India, emergency 112 control rooms receive calls where the person on the line cannot speak freely.  
An intruder in the home. A hostage held under watch. A domestic abuse victim forced to pretend they are ordering a pizza on an emergency frequency.  
Traditional dispatch desks listen only to words. But when danger forces silence... words are the first thing taken away."*

### ACT IV: THE LIVE INVESTIGATION — COVERT DURESS IN ACTION (1:35 – 2:50)

**Visual:**  
Split perspective: Left side shows real mobile phone dialer dialing Extension 966.  
Right side shows the Dhvani Console triggering the live incoming call alert.  
Operator clicks **Answer Call**.  
Header flashes: `Live WebRTC Telephony · Active Stream`. Elapsed timer begins ticking.  
Camera pans dynamically across the 5 intelligence modules:  
- **Module 1 (Speech Biometrics):** Caller speaks *"Hello, I would like to order a large pepperoni pizza to apartment 302."* Pitch jumps to 218 Hz. Jitter and tremor spikes to 52%. Qwen SenseVoice foundation model displays `<|FEARFUL|> [0.94]`.  
- **Module 2 (AI Copilot Guidance):** Shifts to `COVERT DURESS ASSESSMENT`. Protocol prompt warns: *"DO NOT HANG UP. Acknowledge calmly. Switch to binary yes/no questioning."*  
- **Module 3 (Keyword Engine):** Counts repetitions: `1x` $\rightarrow$ `2x` $\rightarrow$ `3+ CONFIRMED · PIZZA DETECTED`. Tactical dispatch alert box springs open in glowing crimson.  
- **Module 4 (Geospatial Pinpoint):** Leaflet Mumbai map automatically zooms into **Dadar West, Apartment 302, 3rd Floor** with pulsing tactical radius.  
- **Module 5 (Background Audio Forensics):** Acoustic DSP detects room echo at 1.2 kHz, aggressor footsteps pacing at 160 Hz, and door latch sounds at 3.4 kHz.  
- **Two-Way Live Translation Tab:** Sub-90ms Marathi speech streams in side-by-side with English translation. Operator clicks `🔒 Yes/No Protocol`, transmitting real-time translated instruction back to the victim's phone.

**Sound Design:**  
Dynamic tension riser. Ticking clock tempo sync. Acoustic spectrum equalizer bounces with real vocal harmonics.

**Narration:**  
*"Watch a live emergency unfold.  
A civilian dials 112 pretending to place a pizza delivery order. An ordinary operator might dismiss it.  
Dhvani immediately isolates the laryngeal micro-tremors in the voice, detecting acute vocal tension. As the caller repeats the food script, the keyword engine flags covert duress, while background DSP detects room reverberation and secondary pacing footsteps.  
The AI Copilot guides the operator to discreet yes-or-no safety protocols, while automatically extracting the spoken address—Dadar West, Apartment 302—and staging tactical dispatch without alerting the perpetrator in the room."*
